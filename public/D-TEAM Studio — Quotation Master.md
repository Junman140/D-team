## 1. How D-TEAM prices a job

**Quote = Equipment (daily rate × days) + Production fee (hourly or flat) + Individual crew + Extras. Minimum engagement: ₦300,000.**

Assumptions used in this document (confirm or correct):

- The production fee covers D-TEAM’s operating crew and video editing. A dedicated director is added at ₦50,000 per day, and sound engineers and lighting are negotiated per job. Equipment is charged separately at the daily rate card. Photographers, designers and content creators are added as individual crew.
- The ₦300,000 minimum applies to full production engagements. À la carte hire of a single item (for example a drone at ₦50,000) follows the rate card.
- Transport is covered by the organizers.
- Internet is provided by an Airtel ODU router at ₦20,000 per day instead of Starlink.
- Printing is quoted separately through a WhatsApp DM to D-TEAM.
- Services outside media (software, network engineering and similar) remain a custom quote.

* D-TEAM provides everything an event needs, including lighting, sound, LED screens, cranes, RED cameras and dollies. These are not inside the packages below. They are quoted per job through a DM to D-TEAM and added on top.
* Every event gets a free sound card and 2 HDMI cables (a ₦15,000 saving, counting the cables at ₦5,000 each). Bookings above ₦4,000,000 also get ₦100,000 off.
